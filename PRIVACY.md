# Privacy

Lumen has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and it never sends your data to its developers.

## Stays on your device

- Your settings, home layout, hidden apps, recent searches and app launch counts are stored only in Lumen's private app storage.
- Notifications, media sessions, contacts and the app list are read on demand to show them on screen. They are not stored or transmitted.
- The accessibility service "Lumen gestures" only performs the lock screen, notifications and screenshot actions. It does not read screen content or what you type.

## Network access

Lumen makes exactly one kind of network request, and only if you turn on **Weather**:

- Your approximate location, rounded to one decimal place (about 10 km), is sent to the
  [Open-Meteo API](https://open-meteo.com/) to fetch the current temperature and conditions.
- No identifiers are sent. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Web, YouTube, Maps, Play Store and settings searches open the corresponding app on your device; Lumen does not send those queries anywhere itself.

## Permissions

Every permission is optional and requested only when you enable the feature that needs it. See the table in the [README](README.md#optional-permissions).

## Removing your data

Clear Lumen's storage in *Settings → Apps → Lumen → Storage*, or uninstall Lumen.
